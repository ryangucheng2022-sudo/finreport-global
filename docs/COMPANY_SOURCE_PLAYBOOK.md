# Company Source Playbook

## TI

- Preferred source: `investor.ti.com`
- Search pattern: `Texas Instruments reports first quarter 2026 financial results revenue`
- Revenue line usually appears in the earnings release summary table.
- Currency: USD.
- Calendar-year reporter.

## Infineon

- Preferred sources: `infineon.com`, `investors.infineon.com`, `eqs-news.com`.
- Search pattern: `Infineon Q2 FY 2026 revenue`.
- Currency: EUR.
- Fiscal year ends September 30.
- For calendar Q1 comparisons, use FY Q2 and label it clearly.

## NXP

- Preferred sources: `investors.nxp.com`, `media.nxp.com`.
- Search pattern: `NXP Semiconductors reports first quarter 2026 results revenue`.
- Currency: USD.
- Calendar-year reporter.

## STM

- Preferred sources: `newsroom.st.com`, `investors.st.com`.
- Search pattern: `STMicroelectronics first quarter 2026 net revenues`.
- Metric name is usually `net revenues`.
- Currency: USD.
- Calendar-year reporter.

## ADI

- Preferred source: `investor.analog.com`.
- Search pattern: `Analog Devices reports fiscal second quarter 2026 financial results revenue`.
- Currency: USD.
- Fiscal year ends around late October/early November.
- For calendar Q1, disclose that FY Q2 is a closest public fiscal-quarter proxy, not strict January-March.

## Renesas

- Preferred source: `renesas.com`.
- Search pattern: `Renesas reports financial results first quarter ended March 31 2026 revenue`.
- Currency: JPY.
- Calendar-year reporter under IFRS.
- Use period-average JPY/USD conversion.

## onsemi

- Preferred sources: `onsemi.com`, `investor.onsemi.com`.
- Search pattern: `onsemi reports first quarter 2026 results revenue`.
- Currency: USD.
- Calendar-year reporter.

## Microchip

- Preferred source: `ir.microchip.com`.
- Search pattern: `Microchip Technology fiscal fourth quarter 2026 revenue`.
- Currency: USD.
- Fiscal year ends March 31.
- For calendar Q1, use fiscal Q4 and label it clearly.

## MPS

- Preferred sources: `monolithicpower.com`, `investor.monolithicpower.com`.
- Search pattern: `Monolithic Power Systems reports first quarter 2026 revenue`.
- Currency: USD.
- Calendar-year reporter.

## Silergy

- Preferred source: MSN Finance in TWD when the required period is published; otherwise `silergy.com/news/financial` monthly USD K.
- The company may publish monthly revenue in USD and/or TWD.
- For quarterly values, sum the three months if a quarterly total is not directly provided.
- If using YTD YoY to infer prior-year values, label it as inferred and prefer raw monthly data when available.
